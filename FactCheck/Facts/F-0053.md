# Viramontes v. Cook County（25-238）+ Grant v. Higgins（25-566），2026-12-02 辩论

## 证据
- 支持：SCOTUSblog 2026-06"Court grants several new cases, including on whether the Second Amendment protects possession of semiautomatic rifles"；Washington Times 2026-06-30"Supreme Court to decide whether states can ban AR-15 rifles"；NRA-ILA 称6-30（上期最后一个令状日）批准。
- 支持：SCOTUSblog 2026-09"Court announces cases it will hear in December, including challenges to … bans on AR-15s"；AmmoLand"Supreme Court Sets Dec. 2 Arguments…as DOJ Backs Gun Owners"；NPR 摘要亦称12月听审。
